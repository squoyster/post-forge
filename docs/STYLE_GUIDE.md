# DKMC Satsang Dawn Style Guide

## Purpose

The goal is not decoration. The goal is to make useful spiritual teaching easier to find, revisit, and practice.

The DKMC page should present:

- practical teachings
- short satsang clips
- live/replay reminders
- reflection prompts
- calendar-aware spiritual reflections

## Visual Direction

Use a warm, calm, devotional style:

- cream/parchment background
- deep purple primary text
- saffron/gold accent lines
- subtle lotus/Om/mandala watermark
- clean mobile-readable typography
- minimal motion

Avoid:

- random stock yoga art
- excessive AI-generated imagery
- unreadable script fonts
- flashy animations
- engagement bait
- generic spiritual quotes not grounded in the source archive

## Format Priorities

1. 1080x1920 vertical video for Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts.
2. 1080x1350 image cards for Facebook feed.
3. 1080x1080 square fallback cards.
4. Text-only posts only when operationally necessary.

## Typography

Display/quote: Cormorant Garamond  
Body/caption: Inter  
Fallback serif: Georgia  
Fallback sans: Arial  

## Caption Rules

Captions must be readable on mobile:

- max 2 lines
- lower third
- high contrast
- phrase-based segmentation
- corrected obvious transcription errors
- no more than ~12 words visible at once

## Editorial Rules

Every generated post must answer:

- Why is this useful?
- Who is this for?
- What source clip/chunk grounds it?
- What should the viewer do next?
